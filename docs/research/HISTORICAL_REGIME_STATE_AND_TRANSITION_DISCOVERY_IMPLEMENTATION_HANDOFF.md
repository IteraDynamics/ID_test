## Observation and candidate contract

One independent observation equals one exact Campaign #46 transition retained by the canonical 168-hour purge.

Confirmatory candidates are exact ordered transition categories:

`<prior_regime> -> <current_regime>`

Candidate rules:

- both labels must be non-`UNKNOWN`;
- self-transitions are excluded;
- candidate IDs are deterministic;
- each category is crossed with horizons `24`, `72`, and `168` hours;
- every category-horizon pair remains visible even when unsupported;
- no learned, merged, thresholded, clustered, or post-result candidate is allowed.

## Frozen controls

For every controlled estimator:

1. trailing 24-hour log return;
2. trailing 72-hour log return;
3. trailing 168-hour log return;
4. trailing 24-hour realized volatility;
5. trailing 168-hour realized volatility;
6. normalized distance from the trailing 168-hour close mean.

All control windows end at the anchor. Exact timestamps are required.

Control scaling:

- arithmetic mean and population standard deviation;
- fit on development observations only;
- applied unchanged to the subsequent evaluation partition;
- zero or non-finite development standard deviation fails closed;
- missing or non-finite controls exclude the affected row from the controlled fit and remain visible with a reason.

## Frozen outcome

`forward_log_return = log(horizon_close / anchor_close)`

Horizons:

- 24 hours;
- 72 hours;
- 168 hours.

Both closes must exist at exact governed timestamps. Maximum adverse excursion and all strategy-performance outcomes remain unauthorized.

## Frozen estimator

For each eligible candidate and horizon:

`forward_log_return ~ intercept + candidate_indicator + six standardized controls`

Implementation requirements:

- ordinary least squares;
- binary candidate indicator;
- candidate absent is the reference level;
- coefficient of interest is the candidate-indicator coefficient;
- HC3 heteroskedasticity-consistent covariance;
- two-sided null test `beta_candidate = 0`;
- two-sided 95% confidence interval;
- no regularization, interactions, nonlinear terms, feature selection, clipping, winsorization, or tuning.

The implementation may use a deterministic in-repository linear algebra path or a fixed dependency already present in the repository. Any dependency/version disagreement that changes numeric output fails closed.

## Frozen support rules

A candidate-horizon fit is rankable only if:

- overall independent support is at least 20;

- candidate-present and candidate-absent counts are each at least 5 in every required estimator sample;
- exact anchors, controls, and horizon closes exist;
- the design matrix is finite and full rank;
- all preflight and leakage checks pass.

A non-rankable candidate remains visible with a deterministic status and reasons.

## Frozen multiplicity contract

Multiplicity family:

- all rankable ordered-transition-by-horizon pooled controlled-estimator tests.

Correction:

- Benjamini-Hochberg;
- false-discovery-rate threshold `q = 0.05`;
- stable sort by raw p-value, then deterministic candidate ID;
- monotone adjusted q-values bounded to `[0, 1]`.

A candidate is confirmatory-supported only when its adjusted q-value is at most `0.05` and its directional-consistency gate passes.

- pooled coefficient has the same sign.

Individual evaluation-fold p-values are descriptive and are not separate significance gates.
