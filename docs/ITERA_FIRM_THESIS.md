# Itera Dynamics Firm Thesis

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term institutional objective that governs Itera Dynamics across campaigns. It is directional context, not implementation authorization.

## End state

Itera Dynamics is building an institutional-grade quantitative investment firm.

The firm will pursue systematic investment decisions through deterministic research, reproducible validation, disciplined portfolio construction, explicit risk governance, controlled execution, and auditable operations.

The research platform is foundational infrastructure for the firm. It is not the final product or the terminal objective.

## Non-negotiable principles

- Deterministic: the same governed inputs and parameters must produce the same outputs.
- Replay-safe: research and decision artifacts must be reproducible and independently verifiable.
- Fail-closed: unavailable, contradictory, unstable, or insufficient evidence must remain visible and must not be silently repaired or promoted.
- Research-to-runtime separation: descriptive and predictive research do not alter live behavior without explicit authorization and independent validation.
- Observation before intervention: research campaigns may measure, classify, test, and falsify without changing thresholds, signals, orders, portfolio state, NAV, or exposure.
- Institutional auditability: data identity, assumptions, transformations, decisions, and outcomes must remain traceable.
- Investment relevance: research rigor exists to improve long-term investment reliability, not to maximize novelty or activity.

## Strategic progression

The intended progression is:

1. governed data and source integrity;
2. deterministic descriptive market structure;
3. predictive-signal discovery and falsification;
4. robust out-of-sample validation;
5. portfolio construction and capital allocation research;
6. risk management and drawdown governance;
7. execution and transaction-cost validation;
8. controlled paper and live deployment;
9. capital formation, compliance, and institutional operations.

This sequence is directional rather than self-authorizing. Each transition requires a separately approved campaign or board decision.

## Campaign alignment rule

Beginning with Campaign #44, every proposed campaign must state:

1. its immediate research or engineering objective;
2. the evidence required for acceptance;
3. its strategic contribution to building Itera Dynamics as a quantitative investment firm;
4. the production, runtime, threshold, signal, order, portfolio, NAV, and exposure changes that remain unauthorized.

A campaign may be strategically important even when it is observation-only or infrastructure-focused. The connection to the institutional end state must nevertheless be explicit.

## Campaign #43 preservation

Campaign #43-R1 remains governed exactly by its frozen specification, R1 amendment, campaign board, and acceptance gates. This document does not change its candidates, sources, anchors, outcomes, horizons, folds, support rules, ranking, outputs, or authorization boundary.

## Success condition

Success is not merely a functioning research platform or a collection of backtests. Success is an investment firm capable of producing, validating, allocating, executing, governing, and explaining systematic investment decisions with institutional discipline.
