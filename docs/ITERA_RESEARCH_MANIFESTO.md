# Itera Research Manifesto

## Knowledge compounds; predictions decay

Itera studies markets before attempting to act on them. Durable understanding is more valuable than an isolated backtest result.

## Observation before prediction

A descriptive result that is reproducible and well-bounded is valid progress. Prediction is not required for research to be useful.

## Taxonomies before optimization

Before asking what should happen next, define what happened, how it is represented, and whether the representation survives replay.

## Determinism creates trust

A result that depends on execution order, operating system, hidden mutable state, or ambiguous serialization cannot become institutional knowledge.

## Every campaign must teach

A completed campaign should create at least one durable answer, reduce a material uncertainty, or improve the reliability of future research.

## Simplicity must be preferred

Use explicit rules, stable ordering, auditable artifacts, and understandable failure modes. Complexity must earn its place through evidence.

## Infrastructure is a multiplier, not the product

Replay, testing, artifact integrity, portability, and documentation are valuable when they protect or accelerate meaningful research. They must not become the default destination of effort without a demonstrated need.

## Production is earned

Confidence precedes capital. Runtime behavior changes only after validation and explicit authorization.

## Failure is information

A rejected hypothesis is useful when the evidence and reasoning are preserved. Undocumented failure is waste; documented failure narrows the search space.

## Institutional memory is deliberate

Important decisions, evidence, and caveats must live in version-controlled artifacts rather than only in conversation or memory.
