# Itera Dynamics Vision

## Purpose

Itera Dynamics exists to build a deterministic quantitative research platform that converts market observations into reproducible, auditable, and reusable knowledge.

Itera is not a signal service, discretionary trading process, or runtime-first trading bot. Research precedes execution. Production may consume validated research, but it does not define research priorities.

## Long-term direction

Itera should compound knowledge across four connected layers:

1. **Observation and research** — describe market behavior without changing production behavior.
2. **Institutional knowledge** — preserve validated definitions, evidence, caveats, and reproducible artifacts.
3. **Strategy and portfolio research** — derive testable sleeves only from validated knowledge.
4. **Runtime execution** — execute explicitly authorized, validated behavior conservatively and fail closed.

## Enduring principles

- Deterministic results from identical inputs.
- Replay-safe and auditable artifacts.
- Observation before prediction.
- Evidence before promotion.
- Explicit separation between research and runtime.
- No hidden mutation of thresholds, orders, NAV, exposure, or production state.
- Negative results remain valuable when documented.

## Definition of progress

Progress is not measured by code volume. Progress means Itera can answer an important question more reliably, reproduce the answer later, and use the result to ask a better next question.
