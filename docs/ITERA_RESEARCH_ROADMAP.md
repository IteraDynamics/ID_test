# Itera Research Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap identifies the sequence of research questions that should compound Itera's knowledge and advance the long-term objective defined in [`ITERA_FIRM_THESIS.md`](ITERA_FIRM_THESIS.md): building an institutional-grade quantitative investment firm.

It is not a feature backlog and does not authorize implementation by itself.

## Roadmap principles

- Research remains specification-first, deterministic, replay-safe, observation-only, and fail-closed unless a campaign explicitly authorizes a later transition.
- Scientific validity takes precedence over speed, narrative, or apparent performance.
- Infrastructure work must protect a concrete research, risk, execution, or institutional dependency.
- The research platform is foundational infrastructure for the firm, not the terminal objective.
- Beginning with Campaign #44, each campaign proposal must state its immediate objective, acceptance evidence, strategic contribution to the quantitative investment firm, and unchanged authorization boundaries.

## Completed foundation

- Deterministic research and replay discipline.
- Historical collapse episode extraction and signature artifacts.
- Core v1 historical regime taxonomy.
- Portable, strict artifact serialization and digest verification.
- Deterministic overlap-aware historical event families.
- Event-family robustness and governed historical evidence reconciliation.

## Active frontier

### Campaign #43-R1 — Core v1 Historical Alpha Discovery

Campaign #43-R1 asks which governed, anchor-available Core v1 historical descriptors exhibit repeatable out-of-sample association with deterministic forward BTC outcomes after overlapping episode windows are corrected through deterministic event families.

Its frozen scope includes governed candidates, exact forward outcomes, fixed horizons, chronological folds, support gates, evidence states, deterministic ranking, canonical outputs, and replay validation.

Campaign #43-R1 must finish exactly as designed. The Itera firm-thesis clarification does not alter its candidates, inputs, methodology, acceptance gates, outputs, or authorization boundary.

Strategic contribution:

- establish whether the governed descriptive taxonomy contains falsifiable predictive information;
- create an auditable bridge from descriptive market structure to predictive-signal research;
- preserve null, contradictory, unstable, insufficient-support, and unavailable results rather than forcing apparent alpha;
- provide evidence that can govern the next research decision without authorizing production use or deployable-alpha claims.

## Strategic sequence after Campaign #43

The exact Campaign #44 scope must be selected from Campaign #43 evidence and separately authorized on the Campaign Board. No result should be presumed before canonical Campaign #43 outputs are generated and inspected.

The directional research-to-firm sequence is:

1. **Predictive falsification and replication** — challenge any Campaign #43 relationships across time, sources, definitions, and independent samples without post-result parameter drift.
2. **Signal specification and calibration** — define candidate signals, confidence, uncertainty, and abstention rules only after predictive evidence survives falsification.
3. **Portfolio construction research** — study diversification, correlation, allocation, concentration, turnover, and capacity under separately frozen assumptions.
4. **Risk governance** — validate drawdown, exposure, liquidity, stress, failure, and kill-switch behavior independently from alpha claims.
5. **Execution and transaction-cost research** — evaluate implementability, slippage, fees, latency, market impact, and venue assumptions using governed evidence.
6. **Controlled deployment** — progress through shadow, paper, and explicitly authorized live stages with independent acceptance gates.
7. **Institutional readiness** — develop capital formation, compliance, reporting, custody, operational controls, and investor-grade auditability.

This sequence is directional, not self-authorizing. Evidence may reorder, repeat, or terminate stages.

## Deferred until explicitly authorized

- learned clustering;
- predictive model training or replacement;
- calibrated production probabilities;
- strategy promotion;
- portfolio or capital-allocation changes;
- runtime integration;
- transaction-cost or capacity claims;
- threshold, signal, intent, order, NAV, or exposure changes;
- deployable-alpha or investment recommendations;
- capital solicitation or live fund operations.

## Roadmap review rule

Update this document when a completed campaign materially changes dependencies, falsifies an assumption, reveals a higher-value next research question, or changes the safest path toward the institutional quantitative investment firm.

Do not rewrite frozen campaign decisions after result inspection. Any changed research decision requires an explicit board transition and separately designated campaign or rerun.
