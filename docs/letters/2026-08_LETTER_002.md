## Research

Two governance items closed this period.

**Benchmark implementation.** The registered comparators moved from specification to running
code: `research/live_benchmarks.py` (deterministic, fail-closed engine),
`scripts/run_core_v1_live_benchmarks.py` (governed runner with replay-identity verification),
and `scripts/run_core_v1_live_comparison.py` (joins the paper runtime's exported NAV against
the benchmark artifacts). Synthetic validation covers 27 tests including hand-computed NAV,
fee, rebalance, alignment, and carry-forward scenarios.

**A defect found and closed before publication.** The first implementation carried a price
source forward at its last known close for any valuation date after that source ended. For an
ordinary non-trading-day gap that is the frozen carry-forward rule; for a materially stale
source it would have silently mispriced a benchmark. The engine now fails closed with
`SOURCE_COVERAGE_FAILURE`. The defect was found during the first governed run and corrected
before any benchmark figure was published. The figures in this letter were verified to be
unaffected: every source carried a session on the requested end date.

## Methodological notes

- The paper series reflects actual fills, fees, and slippage recorded by the runtime; the
  benchmark series apply modeled costs (crypto 6 bps, equity and gold 1 bp) to synthetic
  deployment and monthly rebalancing. Both include costs, but they are not identically
  constructed, and the difference is not adjusted for.
- The benchmark fee model produced 27.49 USD of inception costs against the runtime's 27.36 USD
  of realized costs on comparable notional. This is a favorable but informal reconciliation; it
  is not a formal cost-model validation.
- No rebalance occurred in either benchmark during this window: the first monthly rebalance date
  falls on the first equity session of August, one day past the period end.
